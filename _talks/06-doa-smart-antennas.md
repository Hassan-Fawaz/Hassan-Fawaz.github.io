<div style="text-align: justify;">
My final-year engineering project at the Lebanese University (Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Computer and Communications) focused on direction of arrival estimation for smart antennas in a multi-path environment — the problem of determining the directions from which several wavefronts arrive at an antenna array.
</div>

<div style="text-align: justify;">
Smart antenna arrays and direction of arrival estimation are foundational to modern beamforming, massive MIMO, and the spatial processing used throughout 5G and satellite communication systems today.
</div>
